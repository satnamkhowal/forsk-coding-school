NEW BRANCH WORKFLOW
1. Add one verified record to /branches/data/branches.php.
2. Run: php tools/branch-pages-refresh.php
3. Upload the SEO-named branch image to /branches/images/.
4. Add real employee data to /branches/data/employees.php when available.
5. Confirm the new branch appears in /branches/branches-sitemap.xml.
6. Test the map, phone, address, course links and enrollment form.
